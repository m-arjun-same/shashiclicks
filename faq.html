Booking & payment

How do I book a shoot?

Start with a pre-shoot discussion — an essential first step, usually on phone or WhatsApp. Samples are shown at the studio. The booking advance is ₹5,000, adjusted against your package total. It is fully refunded if you cancel before 72 hours of the shoot; within 72 hours it is non-refundable if cancelled or postponed.

How can I pay?

Cash, Net Banking (RTGS / NEFT / IMPS), Wallet (Paytm), UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay) or Cheque. There is no debit/credit card machine. Payment is due on the day of the shoot.

Can I visit the studio?

By prior appointment only — no walk-ins. Call any day between 10:30 AM and 7 PM on +91 70328 57077. The studio is at 3rd Floor, Hifly Capital, Plot 187, Manjeera Pipeline Rd, Hafeezpet, Miyapur, Hyderabad 500049.

Do you publish package prices?

Pricing is transparent and ready — contact the studio on WhatsApp, call or email and the complete pricing catalog is shared instantly. Additional high-resolution edited photos beyond your package are ₹600 each. Private sessions (photos not shared online) carry a non-usage fee of 40% on package value.

Studio rules

What should we know before we come?

No cell-phone or personal-camera photos or videos are allowed in the studio. Shoes are not allowed inside the studio — please carry a pair to wear indoors; models may wear shoes if the theme requires them. ShashiClicks does not offer passport or visa photography.

Your photos

When do we receive our photos?

Originals are ready 1 week after the session / invoice closing. Edited images are delivered within 3 weeks of your selection, via a cloud link sent by email (about 30 days to download — a computer is recommended, as the files are large). "Rush my Order" delivers in 10 days, excluding prints and products. Prints are ready in 1 week; albums take up to 4 weeks (domestic) or 10 weeks (international). Client data is stored for 90 days from the shoot.

What retouching is included?

Colour correction, light correction and basic skin retouching. The studio does not offer photo manipulation, background replacement, changing physical appearance, or adding elements.

Do the photos carry a logo?

All edited images carry the ShashiClicks logo in one of the four corners (the position is not choosable); unedited images have no logo. Logo-free images can be purchased through the client form option. Prints are held for 2 weeks only, then discarded without refund — studio pickup is preferred.

Who owns the copyright?

Under the Indian Copyright Act 1957, copyright stays with the photographer; clients may buy the copyright through the client form. The logo may not be removed or cropped out. For costume-provided shoots (newborn / maternity), ShashiClicks may share images online unless you opt out in the booking form notes.

Maternity sessions

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

Between 30–36 weeks — book 2–3 weeks ahead. Each look or dress takes 30–45 minutes. Maternity gowns and a wide prop range are provided (usage per package). Hair & makeup is not included, and outside makeup artists are not allowed in the studio. Spouse and children are welcome. Outdoor shoots happen at sunrise or in the hour before sunset; studio sessions run 11 AM – 8 PM, with no weekend surcharge. Stretch marks and blemishes are retouched; your shortlist link arrives about a week after the shoot and edited delivery takes 3–4 weeks.

Locations & gear

Where can we shoot?

All packages include studio shooting at no extra charge. Preferred locations: the ShashiClicks Studio (indoor), Shri Kotla Vijaybhaskar Reddy Botanical Garden, Jalamandali Park and Ramoji Film City. Location and garden charges are extra, paid by the client at current rates.

What gear does the studio use?

Canon R5 Mark II, Canon R5, Canon 5D Mark III, Canon EOS 7D and Nikon Coolpix P100, with Canon RF 70-200mm f/2.8L IS USM, RF 28-70mm F2 L USM, RF 50mm f/1.4L VCM and EF 70-200mm f/2.8L USM lenses. The gear is not for sale or rent.
