The Founders

Shashi Patel

Founder — NIT Raipur

“I am Shashi — an NITian by qualification, an artist at heart, and the founder of ShashiClicks®. My love affair with photography began in childhood with a Kodak reel camera and followed me through my engineering days at NIT Raipur. Realizing that freezing moments was my true calling, I chose passion over a corporate desk. For me, every single click isn't just a service; it's a timeless memory.”

Our vision — “To click every shot as our favourite for life time!”

Neelam Patel

Co-Founder & Studio Manager

An engineer by profession and photographer by passion, with over 9 years' experience in the professional photography industry. Winner of the Telangana State Photography Award and corporate & national level awards. Her job is primarily client care and communications — if you call the studio phone, she's who you'll speak with. Clients call her the “Newborn Whisperer”.

Our mission — “To capture stories in the form of images, so you can look back years from today and relive that moment in time.”
