The founders

Shashi Patel

Founder — NIT Raipur

“I am Shashi — an NITian by qualification, an artist at heart, and the founder of ShashiClicks®. My love affair with photography began in childhood with a Kodak reel camera and followed me through my engineering days at NIT Raipur. Realizing that freezing moments was my true calling, I chose passion over a corporate desk.
